# **Plate Studio**

## 
Small restaurants rarely have the time or budget to film a video ad. Plate Studio turns a Google Maps or Yelp link and a few phone photos of your dishes into a finished, voiced and scored ad you can publish anywhere. The hardest part was building a full video pipeline that still renders a polished ad even when an AI service fails or runs out of credits. Check out the [landing page](https://github.com/JacobQuion/Plate-Studio-Landing) built to market it.

## **Highlights**
- AI assistant powered by Claude that edits the ad through tools, so owners can just say "make it punchier" or "longer ramen shot" instead of using an editor.
- Custom FFmpeg pipeline that animates every dish photo, adds cooking and first bite shots, voiceover, music and captions, then joins it all with a warm film grade.
- Clickable preview and scene timeline where owners can edit text, reorder dishes and change camera moves or transitions directly on the video.
- Built-in fallbacks for every stage plus one click publishing to YouTube, Instagram, TikTok and Meta in 16:9, 9:16 or 1:1.
